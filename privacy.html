Privacy Statements

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

At What’s The Tee? ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we manage and process your Personal Data in connection with our business, website, Online Shop, Products, Services and other interactions with you.

1. Information We Collect

What’s The Tee? may collect Personal Data that is necessary to process your Orders, provide our Services, improve our Products, and maintain the functionality of our website. Thus, we may use your data for the following purposes:

  • To process, confirm, and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations, acknowledgement receipts, and delivery updates.
  • To reply to your questions and messages sent through our Contact page.
  • To improve our Products, Services, and website.
  • To follow legal and regulatory requirements.

The Personal Data we collect may include your name, e-mail address, telephone number, billing information, delivery address, the products, colorway, size, and quantity you order, and the messages you send us. We also collect basic website data such as your browser type, device, and the pages you visit.

2. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your Personal Data. We only share it with people who help us run our business, such as courier and delivery partners and payment providers, and only the information they need. We may also share your Personal Data if the law or a government authority requires it.

3. Data Retention

We keep your Personal Data only for as long as we need it to complete your Orders, handle returns or concerns, and follow our legal and record-keeping duties. After that, we delete it or make it anonymous.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your Personal Data from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No website is completely secure, but we take care of the information you trust us with.

5. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your Personal Data is collected and used.
  • Access the Personal Data we hold about you.
  • Correct Personal Data that is wrong or out of date.
  • Object to the use of your Personal Data, or ask us to block or delete it.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you think your rights were violated.

To use any of these rights, e-mail us at hello@examplewebsite142.com.

6. Cookies and Other Services

Our website loads fonts and design files from other providers (like Google Fonts and jsDelivr). They may receive basic information, such as your IP address, when your browser loads those files. Our website may also use cookies so it can work properly. You can turn cookies off in your browser settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your Personal Data, e-mail us at hello@examplewebsite142.com.
